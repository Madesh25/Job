Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Job Engine ("the app", shown on the Google sign-in screen as "Job") is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Madeshwaran Manikam, who uses it with his own Google accounts for his own job search.

Data the app accesses

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): reads job alerts, application confirmations, rejections and replies; adds labels; writes drafts; sends mails the owner has approved.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): only files the app creates itself, such as resume documents.

How the data is used

  • To track the owner's job applications and keep their status up to date.
  • To prepare and send job-search mails on the owner's behalf, after his approval.
  • Short parts of mail text (for example a subject line and a few sentences) may be sent to AI model providers (Google, Anthropic, DeepSeek) to sort a reply, for example "interview" or "rejection". These providers process the text only to return that answer.

Storage and sharing

  • Results are kept in the owner's own Notion workspace and his own Google Cloud project.
  • Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and in his Google Cloud Secret Manager.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Job Engine's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The owner can delete the stored data from his Notion workspace and Google Cloud project at any time.
