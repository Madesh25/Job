Job Engine

Job Engine (shown on the Google sign-in screen as "Job") is a personal job-search assistant built and used by one person, Madeshwaran Manikam, for his own job search. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Finds DevOps job postings and keeps them in the owner's own Notion workspace.
  • Reads job alerts, application confirmations and replies in the owner's own Gmail account, so the status of each application stays up to date.
  • Writes mail drafts in the owner's own Gmail account, and sends only mails the owner has approved.
  • Saves resume files it creates in the owner's own Google Drive.

Google account access

The app asks for Gmail access (to read, label and draft mails) and for access to Drive files it creates itself. It is used only with the owner's own Google accounts.
