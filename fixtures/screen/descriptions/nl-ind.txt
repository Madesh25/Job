Canal Payments processes card payments for European merchants. You will join a small team that owns the delivery platform used by about forty product engineers. The team runs on-call in a fair rotation, writes blameless post-mortems and spends a fixed share of every sprint on reducing toil. We document decisions in short design notes and review each other's infrastructure changes through merge requests. New joiners get a buddy for the first months, a training budget and time to learn the systems before joining the on-call rotation. You have 3+ years of experience running Kubernetes and Terraform on AWS. Experience with Grafana dashboards is welcome. This is a permanent position. Hybrid in Amsterdam. Salary EUR 62,000 - 74,000 per year. You will be improving the availability of the payment gateway.
