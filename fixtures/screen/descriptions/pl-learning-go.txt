Odra Systems is building internal developer tooling. You will join a small team that owns the delivery platform used by about forty product engineers. The team runs on-call in a fair rotation, writes blameless post-mortems and spends a fixed share of every sprint on reducing toil. We document decisions in short design notes and review each other's infrastructure changes through merge requests. New joiners get a buddy for the first months, a training budget and time to learn the systems before joining the on-call rotation. You write production services in Go every day and run them on Kubernetes. 3 years of experience. Employment contract (UoP) or B2B.
