The team runs on-call in a fair rotation, writes blameless post-mortems and spends a fixed share of every sprint on reducing toil. You have 3 years of commercial experience with AWS and Terraform, and you like Linux. Cooperation is based on a B2B contract only, 150 - 180 PLN net per hour. Remote work from anywhere in Poland. English is required for daily work.
