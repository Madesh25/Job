Vistula Cloud is hiring a DevOps Engineer in Kraków. You will join a small team that owns the delivery platform used by about forty product engineers. The team runs on-call in a fair rotation, writes blameless post-mortems and spends a fixed share of every sprint on reducing toil. We document decisions in short design notes and review each other's infrastructure changes through merge requests. New joiners get a buddy for the first months, a training budget and time to learn the systems before joining the on-call rotation. Requirements: at least 3 years of experience in a DevOps role. Kubernetes in production. Terraform for all infrastructure as code. Good knowledge of AWS. Experience with Prometheus is a plus. English at B2 level is required. We offer an employment contract (umowa o prace, UoP). Hybrid work from our Kraków office. Salary 16 000 - 21 000 PLN gross per month. You will be moving our workloads to EKS and running GitOps with Argo CD for forty services.
