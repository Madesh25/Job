Liffey Recruitment is recruiting on behalf of our client, a growing software company in Dublin. You will join a small team that owns the delivery platform used by about forty product engineers. The team runs on-call in a fair rotation, writes blameless post-mortems and spends a fixed share of every sprint on reducing toil. We document decisions in short design notes and review each other's infrastructure changes through merge requests. New joiners get a buddy for the first months, a training budget and time to learn the systems before joining the on-call rotation. The client needs 3 years of AWS and Terraform experience and solid Linux skills. Permanent role, hybrid working.
