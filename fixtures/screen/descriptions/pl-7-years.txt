Description source: company site
Vistula Cloud is growing its SRE group in Wrocław. You will join a small team that owns the delivery platform used by about forty product engineers. The team runs on-call in a fair rotation, writes blameless post-mortems and spends a fixed share of every sprint on reducing toil. We document decisions in short design notes and review each other's infrastructure changes through merge requests. New joiners get a buddy for the first months, a training budget and time to learn the systems before joining the on-call rotation. You have at least 7 years of experience in operations, with Kubernetes and Terraform. Employment contract (UoP). Hybrid work.
