Baltic Bank is hiring a Platform Engineer in Warszawa. You will join a small team that owns the delivery platform used by about forty product engineers. The team runs on-call in a fair rotation, writes blameless post-mortems and spends a fixed share of every sprint on reducing toil. We document decisions in short design notes and review each other's infrastructure changes through merge requests. New joiners get a buddy for the first months, a training budget and time to learn the systems before joining the on-call rotation. You bring 4 years of hands-on work with Kubernetes, Helm and GitLab CI. Knowledge of Java is nice to have. Polish is a plus, English is required. Employment contract (UoP). Hybrid, two days a week in the office. You will be building self-service environments for product teams.
